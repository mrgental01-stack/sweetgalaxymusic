About us

Sweet Galaxy Productions

Sweet Galaxy Productions is the music label and beat studio of producer MrGental from Zaandam, the Netherlands.

We create beats with a warm Caribbean heartbeat: dancehall riddims, shatta, hip-hop, R&B and Latin vibes, ready for artists who want to stand out.

Every beat is professionally mixed and available as an affordable lease, so you can release your music fast and keep 100% of your vocals and creativity.
